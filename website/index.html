Write it. Find it. Ask it.

Margin is a note-taking and PDF app that feels like paper, and answers questions from what you wrote. Its AI can run entirely on your own device.

No account. No subscription. How to install

Writes like paper.

Pressure and tilt shape the line, and your palm can rest on the page. Scratch something out to erase it. Finish a stroke and hold still, and it becomes a clean line, circle or box.

Marks up any PDF.

Bring in textbooks, slides and papers. Highlight, underline and circle right on the page, add sticky notes and stamps, then export the whole thing as a PDF to share.

Answers from your notes.

Ask a question and get the answer from what you wrote. Turn any note into a summary, flashcards with spaced review, a quiz or a mind map, or explain a topic and have Margin check your understanding.

A private model runs on your device. Add your own NVIDIA key for a stronger one, and Margin falls back to the device whenever NVIDIA can't answer.

Keeps everything in reach.

Folders, tags, notebook covers, templates and tabs for the notes you have open. Search reaches your typed text, PDFs and handwriting. Record a lecture, then tap what you wrote to hear what was being said at that moment.

Install in a minute

Android

  1. Download the APK on your device.
  2. Open it, and allow your browser to install apps when asked.
  3. For offline AI, open Settings and download the writing model.

Android 10 or later, 64-bit. The on-device AI needs 4 GB of memory or more.

iPad

  1. Download the IPA on a Mac or Windows PC.
  2. Install it with Sideloadly or AltStore, signing in with your Apple ID.
  3. For offline AI, open Settings and download the writing model.

iPadOS 17 or later. Margin isn't on the App Store yet, and with a free Apple ID it has to be reinstalled every 7 days.

Questions

Is Margin free?

Yes. There is no subscription and no account. The optional online AI uses your own NVIDIA API key.

Does the AI work offline?

Yes. Download the 1.1 GB on-device model once from Settings, and questions are answered on your device with nothing sent anywhere.

Where do my notes go?

They stay on your device, and in your own iCloud Drive folder or Google backup if you use one. If you add an NVIDIA key, your question and the notes it needs are sent to NVIDIA to answer it.

Do I need a stylus?

No. You can write with a finger. With a stylus you also get pressure, optional tilt and palm rejection.

Are the iPad and Android apps the same?

The core is: ink, PDFs, recording and the study tools. The iPad app is further along, with live transcripts, Live Text, iCloud sync, more paper styles and tools such as signatures and study tape. Notes don't move between the two yet.
